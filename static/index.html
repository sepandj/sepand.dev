the moment you arrived, this page was made for you.

every visit ever made here is one thread. they are drawn oldest first, and yours comes last, the bright one. its path comes from a fingerprint sealed to your visit and chained to every visit before it. no one else will be given it, and you won't be given it again.

so the weave only ever grows. once in a long while a rare visit arrives and opens a new era, with its own currents and colours, and every thread after it is different.

the circles the threads curl around are doors to the other things i make. each new door bends every thread that comes after it.

keep it, or share it. the link will always open this one.
